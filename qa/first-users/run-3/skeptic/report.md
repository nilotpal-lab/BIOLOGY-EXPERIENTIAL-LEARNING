## Trust verdict

I came in expecting the usual demo-app overclaiming and left mostly convinced. Every number I saw is tied to a disclosed rule: the methodology page publishes the exact cascade-eligibility rules, metric formulas with divide-by-zero guards, and a validation suite that visibly runs in the browser (70/70 passing when I re-ran it). Research datasets carry full academic provenance — author-year citations, Dryad DOIs, related-article DOIs, CC0 1.0 licence text, source file paths, and node-unit caveats — and the two replicate mesocosm ponds are explicitly flagged as non-independent. The demo networks are labelled "Illustrative demo… not a verified empirical food-web dataset" everywhere they appear, and the app repeatedly states what it does NOT claim (no extinction prediction, no population dynamics, no single vulnerability score). The sharpest test I could run — importing fabricated data with a self-declared fake citation — produced the honest status "Research source provided, validation pending", never "verified", and my own data was removable with confirmation and stayed gone after refresh. Weak spots: one dead navigation button (Experiment Studio), no app-level terms/contact page, and validation fixtures ("VALIDATION-…") are visible in the production network dropdown, which slightly muddies an otherwise clean data library. For coursework I would cite this tool's outputs from the RES-*/MAR-* verified networks, with their citations and the "structural, not extinction" caveat; I would not cite anything from the demo networks. Tooling note: console/network capture was not available — not tested.
